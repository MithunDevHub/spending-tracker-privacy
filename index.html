Spending Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Spending Tracker ("the App") is a personal expense tracking application designed to help users record, organize, and review their financial transactions.

1. Information We Collect

Spending Tracker does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, address, or phone number to use the App.

Transaction information entered into the App, such as income, expenses, categories, amounts, dates, and related details, is stored locally on the user's device for the functionality of the App.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored within the App is used to provide features such as transaction tracking, spending summaries, insights, categories, dashboard information, and transaction exports.

3. Data Sharing

Spending Tracker does not sell, rent, or share your transaction information with third parties.

The App does not transmit your transaction information to a developer operated server as part of its normal V1 functionality.

4. Exporting Your Data

The App provides an option to export transaction information. When you choose to export your data, the exported file is handled according to the destination and storage options selected by you on your device.

5. Permissions

The App may use device permissions where required by specific functionality. Permissions are requested only when needed by the corresponding feature.

6. Data Security

Since transaction information is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device, operating system, and device access controls.

7. Children's Privacy

Spending Tracker is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
